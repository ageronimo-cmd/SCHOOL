How the school day actually runs

A mix of core skills, hands-on subjects, and unstructured time — balanced across the week, not crammed into one long day.

Grades K–2

Foundations

Phonics, number sense, and a lot of read-aloud time. Mornings are quiet and focused; afternoons are for building and play.

Grades 3–4

Projects & practice

Math and writing skills deepen alongside term-long projects — a garden log, a class newspaper, a bridge-building unit.

Grades 5–6

Bridge to middle school

More independence, a first research paper, and study habits that carry students into a bigger school with confidence.
